Likes are about 4.5% of views, while saves and shares are concentrated in a few posts.

- Likes: 26,479
- Saves: 7,373, with 93% from the two top posts
- Shares: 5,653, with 93% from the two top posts

Without the top two posts, saves and shares are low and comments stay thin.
